What cleaning services do you provide?

Since 1976, we have provided commercial cleaning services to many different industries. We understand that every business is unique and has different needs. Reliable Cleaning offers janitorial/custodial services, carpet and floor cleaning, window washing, trash removal, disinfecting, and more.

What property types do you serve?

We specialize in large to medium sized facilities. Manufacturing facilities, Industrial facilities, warehouse distribution centers, corporate offices, call centers, medical facilities, colleges/schools campuses, auto dealerships, government buildings and municipalities, and public venues. Our ideal job is cleaning five days a week or more. Our ideal customer cares about cleaning and has set a cleaning standard for their facility.

How does your pricing compare to other commercial cleaning companies?

Reliable Cleaning may not be the least expensive cleaning company, however, we are committed to providing high quality commercial cleaning and disinfection services to every single customer. With our consistent quality cleaning, commitment to ongoing training, quality assurance procedures, and a strong emphasis on communication and accountability, you'll find you get the most for your money.

How do you ensure smooth job startup?

We know it can be worrisome to hire a new vendor, especially one who regularly enters your building and provides cleaning services. Rest assured, we have a complete multi functional team that works hard to start jobs smoothly. Current clients have expressed their satisfaction with our work because when an issue arises, we work quickly to solve the problem so you can get back to what you do while we handle the cleaning.

How does Reliable Cleaning foster long term partnerships?

Reliable Cleaning's proven process fosters long term partnerships with a dedicated and responsive manager assigned to your account for client visits, day and evening inspections, Quarterly Business Reviews, verifying service quality and fine tuning service as needs change.

How do you strive for a skilled and loyal workforce?

Our Skill training includes in house training, on the job training, Video training with quizzes, 30 day check ins, and refresher training. Loyalty comes from Helping our Employees Succeed and offering some of the best pay rates, benefits and opportunities in our industry.
