## Registration and derivatives
The original night and morning generations changed geometry slightly. They were rejected as direct overlay layers. The published night and morning WebP files retain the base illustration raster geometry and transfer the corresponding generated lighting palettes into that geometry. Night uses a fitted color transform; morning uses monotonic color lookups. This changes color, never coordinates. The overlays were inspected before the room masks were built. The unused PNG generations are retained for provenance and excluded from deployment.
Room crops use identical normalized coordinates in every state. The six crops form the phone stack; building-tall-busy.webp, building-tall-night.webp and building-tall-morning.webp record the matching 9:16 layouts.
The marker replacement is cropped and its solid night background removed for transparency. The first blueprint had architecture and was rejected; the replacement is a grid only.
