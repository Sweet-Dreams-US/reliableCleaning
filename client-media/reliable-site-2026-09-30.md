## The Proven Process (their graphic, client-media/site/Updated-Font-Size-1024x576.png)
"The Proven Process Customers Can Expect." Business Development works with the client through the first four; the Area Manager takes care of the client from Startup on.
1. Introduction: About You; About Us; Walkthrough Cleaning Areas; Needs Analysis; ID service hot spots or prioritization.
2. Proposal: Delivered; Discussed; Modified; Signed.
3. Planning: 30+ days; Introduction to Account Manager.
4. Startup: First clean; Fine tune; Address issues; Adjust scope.
5. Ensure Service Level: Client visits; Daytime Inspections; Evening Inspections; Quarterly Business Review; Cross Training.
6. Additional Services: Windows; Floors; Consumables; Construction clean.
7. Annual Review: Service quality; Future needs; Fine tuning; Agreement renewal. (Then back to Ensure Service Level.)
