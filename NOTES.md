## Empty on purpose

Published email, office hours, crew photographs, and locally rebuilt blog articles. No public notices or blank sections. No example requests. The admin notification email is a future live-site setting; no email delivery or server submission exists in this static build.

## Implementation

Plain HTML files for every requested route, self hosted Geologica, Onest and Martian Mono, vendored GSAP and ScrollTrigger. The home night shift pins for 300vh, uses six registered room crops and a travelling bubble marker, and ends at 6 AM. Phone uses the same room crops in a vertical stack. Reduced motion shows the labelled finished state.

Higgsfield supplied the base drawing, lighting references, marker, blueprint and page title art. Raw lighting generations had geometric drift. The published lighting layers transfer those generated palettes onto the original raster geometry, keeping every object fixed. The manifest records prompts, rejected assets and derivatives.

Quote forms and admin edits use localStorage, on the same browser and origin. Quote status, notes and CSV export work. Services, industries, testimonials, news, FAQ, About, business information, announcement and photos have admin screens. The admin has no login and no public navigation link.
